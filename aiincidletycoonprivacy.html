Privacy Policy for AI Inc: Idle Tycoon

Effective Date: 10 October 2026
Last Updated: 10 October 2026

1. About This Policy

This policy explains how information is handled in the Android version of AI Inc: Idle Tycoon. In this policy, “we” refers to the game's developer, who can be contacted at tzveta675@gmail.com.

The game stores progress on your device and integrates Google's advertising and consent services. The current version does not provide player accounts or a developer-operated cloud-save service. This policy is an explanation of data handling; it does not replace the separate privacy choices presented through the consent system.

2. Local Game Data

The game saves information on your device, including:

  • Business levels, in-game money, Tokens, upgrades and AI Agents.
  • Achievements, XP, progression counters and tutorial history.
  • Production timing, offline reward claims and temporary boosts.
  • Preferences such as sound effects and haptics.

This information lets the game restore progress, calculate offline production, remember settings and prevent rewards from being collected more than once. The game's save system does not upload this save file to a developer-operated server.

Local progress remains on the device until the app's stored data is removed or replaced. You can remove local app data through Android's app settings. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data. These actions can permanently remove your progress. A Tokens Restart resets the current run while preserving permanent progression; it is not a complete data-deletion tool.

3. Google Advertising Services

The game uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to offer optional rewarded advertisements. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising privacy choices.

Advertising-related data can include IP addresses, approximate location inferred from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID when available.

Google uses advertising data to deliver and measure ads, analyze service performance and prevent fraud. Personalization and data handling depend on privacy choices, device settings and Google's applicable advertising configuration.

An ad may be loaded in advance once UMP permits ad requests, before you choose to watch it. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad therefore does not mean that no advertising-related network activity has occurred.

AdMob's own measurement and diagnostic functions are part of the advertising integration. The current game does not integrate separate Firebase Analytics, GameAnalytics or Unity Ads services.

4. Consent and Privacy Options

At startup, the game requests updated consent information through UMP and presents a privacy form when required. UMP handles the consent information and choices used by Google's advertising services. The game checks whether UMP permits ad requests before loading or displaying rewarded ads.

When UMP requires a privacy-options entry point, you can open Settings > Privacy Options in the game to review or change the available choices. This entry may not appear when UMP reports that it is not required.

Permission to request an ad does not necessarily mean that you agreed to personalized advertising. Non-personalized or limited advertising may still involve data processing. The choices available depend on the privacy message presented to you.

You can also use Android's advertising privacy controls to reset or delete your advertising ID where supported. Device controls vary by Android version and do not replace the in-game privacy choices.

Accepting the game's Terms or reading this policy is not consent to personalized advertising. Where a consent choice is required, it is handled separately through the privacy form.

5. Information You Send to Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include, such as your name, message, screenshots or diagnostic details. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue you report. Please avoid sending passwords or other information that is unnecessary for your request.

Contact us for information about how long support correspondence is retained or to request deletion of correspondence containing your personal information.

6. Third-Party Privacy Information

Google processes information through its advertising and consent services. Advertiser websites or apps that you visit after interacting with an ad have their own privacy practices.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services
  • Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures
  • Google User Messaging Platform information

These policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting them involves a connection to GitHub's hosting service, which handles visitor information under its own Privacy Statement . These documents do not include analytics scripts, advertising scripts or tracking pixels.

7. Retention, Storage and Security

Gameplay saves are stored locally as described above. Google handles retention and deletion of information processed through its services according to its policies and available privacy controls. Removing the game's local data does not automatically delete information already held by Google or an advertiser.

Google may process advertising information in countries other than your own. Google's policy explains its international data handling. The game does not provide a developer-operated international cloud-save service.

No device storage or network service can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, request portability, or withdraw consent where processing depends on consent. You may also have the right to raise a complaint with a relevant data-protection authority.

For requests concerning information you have sent to the developer, contact tzveta675@gmail.com. For information processed by Google, its privacy policy provides relevant controls and request options. Use Settings > Privacy Options when available to change advertising privacy choices.

9. Children and Age Requirements

The game is intended for users aged 13 and above, consistent with its Terms & Conditions. It is not intended for children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about information relating to a child, contact us at tzveta675@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the game or its data handling. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Advertising consent, when required, remains a separate choice handled through the privacy system.
