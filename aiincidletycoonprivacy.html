Privacy Policy for AI Inc: Idle Tycoon

Effective Date: 10 October 2026
Last Updated: 10 October 2026

1. About This Policy

This policy explains how the Android version of AI Inc: Idle Tycoon handles information when you play the game, use its advertising features or contact its developer. In this policy, “we” means the developer of AI Inc: Idle Tycoon, reachable through the contact details below.

The game stores gameplay progress locally and integrates Google AdMob for rewarded advertisements and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising privacy choices. This policy is an explanation of those practices; using the game does not, by itself, constitute consent to personalized advertising.

2. Local Gameplay Data

The game saves information in its local app storage so you can continue playing. This includes:

  • Business levels, in-game currency, upgrades and AI Agent ownership.
  • Tokens, restart history, achievements and player XP.
  • Production timing, offline reward claims and temporary boost timers.
  • Sound, haptics and tutorial or notification preferences and history.

The current game does not require a game account or provide a developer-operated cloud-save service. Its gameplay save file is not uploaded by the game to a developer server. Advertising SDK data is separate from this local save and is described below.

Local save data remains on your device until it is removed. On Android, you can clear the game’s app storage through your device settings or uninstall the app to remove its local data. Device menu names vary. Clearing storage removes progress and preferences and may also clear locally stored SDK settings. It does not delete information already held by Google.

A Tokens Restart resets the current run while retaining specified permanent progress and preferences. It is not a complete privacy-data deletion function.

3. Google AdMob and Advertising Data

The game offers optional rewarded advertisements through Google AdMob. These can provide Profit Boost, Speed Boost, Offline Earnings x2, AI Opportunity or Token Restart rewards.

Google’s advertising SDK can collect and share information when advertising services operate. Google documents the following categories for its Android advertising SDK:

Information Examples
Network and general location information IP address, which can be used to estimate a device’s general location.
Interactions App launches, taps, advertisement interactions and video views.
Diagnostics Information about app and SDK performance.
Identifiers Android advertising ID, app set ID and other applicable device or account identifiers.

Google uses advertising data for purposes such as delivering advertisements, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. The information processed depends on SDK behavior, applicable settings and your privacy choices.

After UMP permits advertising requests, the game can preload a rewarded advertisement before you choose to watch it. Advertising requests and associated data processing can therefore occur even if you do not press a rewarded-ad button.

Choosing not to receive personalized advertisements does not necessarily prevent all advertising data processing. Google may provide other advertising modes where permitted.

The current game has no separate Firebase, GameAnalytics or Unity Ads integration. Google AdMob’s own measurement and diagnostic processing still applies.

4. Consent and Privacy Options

The game uses Google UMP to update advertising consent information at startup and display a privacy message when required. UMP handles privacy choices and consent state for Google’s advertising services.

The game checks whether UMP permits ad requests before loading or displaying an advertisement. If that permission is unavailable, rewarded advertisements remain unavailable. Permission to request an advertisement does not necessarily mean you agreed to personalized advertisements.

When UMP indicates that a privacy-options entry is required, open the game’s Settings → Privacy Options to review or change available choices. This entry may be hidden when UMP does not require it. Available choices depend on the privacy message and your region.

You can also review Android’s advertising-ID controls in your device settings. Availability and menu names depend on your Android version. Device advertising controls do not replace the game’s UMP privacy choices.

The sound and haptics switches control game feedback; they do not change advertising consent.

7. Storage, Retention and Security

Gameplay saves are stored locally as described above. Google’s retention, deletion and international processing practices are described in its privacy policy; clearing your game save does not erase Google’s records.

No storage system or internet connection is completely secure. This policy does not promise that local data can always be recovered or that third-party processing is risk-free.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, or to withdraw consent. You may also have the right to raise a concern with a data protection authority.

Contact us using the details below for requests concerning information held by the developer. For information held by Google, consult Google’s privacy policy and privacy controls. We do not have remote access to your device’s local save file.

Where processing is based on consent, withdrawing that consent does not undo processing that occurred before withdrawal.
