Privacy Policy for AI Inc: Idle Tycoon

Effective Date: 10 October 2026
Last Updated: October 13, 2026

1. About This Policy

This policy explains how information is handled in the Android version of AI Inc: Idle Tycoon, including version 1.1.0. In this policy, “we” refers to the game's developer, who can be contacted at tzveta675@gmail.com.

The game stores progress on your device and integrates Google's advertising and consent services, operational Firebase Remote Config, and optional Firebase Analytics. The current version does not provide player accounts or a developer-operated cloud-save service. This policy is an explanation of data handling; it does not replace the separate privacy choices presented through the consent system.

2. Local Game Data

The game saves information on your device, including:

  • Business levels, in-game money, Tokens, upgrades and AI Agents.
  • Achievements, XP, progression counters and tutorial history.
  • Production timing, offline reward claims and temporary boosts.
  • Preferences such as sound effects and haptics.

This information lets the game restore progress, calculate offline production, remember settings and prevent rewards from being collected more than once. The game's save system does not upload this save file to a developer-operated server.

Local progress remains on the device until the app's stored data is removed or replaced. You can remove local app data through Android's app settings. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data. These actions can permanently remove your progress. A Tokens Restart resets the current run while preserving permanent progression; it is not a complete data-deletion tool.

3. Google Advertising Services

The game uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to offer optional rewarded advertisements. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) manages advertising privacy choices.

Advertising-related data can include IP addresses, approximate location inferred from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID when available.

Google uses advertising data to deliver and measure ads, analyze service performance and prevent fraud. Personalization and data handling depend on privacy choices, device settings and Google's applicable advertising configuration.

An ad may be loaded in advance after the startup advertising-availability check permits ads and UMP permits ad requests, before you choose to watch it. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad therefore does not mean that no advertising-related network activity has occurred.

AdMob's own measurement and diagnostic functions are part of the advertising integration and are separate from optional Firebase Analytics. Turning off Firebase Analytics does not turn off AdMob's measurement or diagnostic processing. The game does not integrate GameAnalytics or Unity Ads services.

4. Optional Firebase Analytics

AI Inc: Idle Tycoon uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how players use the game and to improve gameplay, balance and the player experience. This analytics collection is optional and requires your explicit opt-in consent. You can decline and continue playing.

Information collected when enabled

Analytics may include:

  • Game sessions and gameplay duration.
  • Business unlocks, upgrades and progression.
  • Funding or prestige activity.
  • Rewarded-ad interactions, reward outcomes and related gameplay events.
  • App and device information, such as app version, device model and operating system; approximate location derived from network information; and pseudonymous identifiers such as an app-instance ID.

We do not intentionally include player names, email addresses or other directly identifying personal information in our custom gameplay analytics events. Pseudonymous identifiers and associated usage information may still be personal information.

Defaults and withdrawal

On a fresh installation, Firebase Analytics is disabled and its consent settings for analytics storage, advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization default to denied. Firebase Analytics is not initialized while your analytics choice is unknown or has been declined.

If you opt in, the game grants analytics-storage consent before enabling collection. Firebase's advertising-storage, advertising-user-data and advertising-personalization consent settings remain denied. These Firebase settings do not determine your separate AdMob advertising choices.

You can withdraw analytics consent through the game's privacy settings. The game immediately stops sending new gameplay analytics events, disables Firebase Analytics collection, changes its consent settings to denied, and resets Analytics data stored locally on your device, including the app-instance identifier. Your choice is saved and restored before Firebase Analytics initialization on later launches.

Withdrawal and the local reset do not automatically delete data already transmitted to Google. Previously collected information remains subject to the retention rules and deletion options described below.

5. Firebase Remote Config

The Android game uses Google Firebase Remote Config to retrieve the operational adsEnabled setting, which controls whether advertisements are available. The game attempts to fetch and activate this setting at each app startup. Ad initialization, loading and display wait until the startup check completes or times out. If the request fails or times out, the game uses the last activated setting, or an enabled default if no activated value is available. Ads also remain subject to UMP's permission to request them.

Operational information processed

Remote Config and Firebase Installations generate and process a pseudonymous Firebase installation identifier and installation authentication information. They also process app and technical metadata, such as app identity and version, operating-system and SDK versions, language and locale country settings, time zone, device information and app installation-source information. The locale country setting is a device language/region setting, not a GPS location reading. Google also handles technical connection information, including IP addresses, when providing Firebase services.

A Firebase installation identifier is different from an Analytics app-instance identifier, an advertising identifier or a player account identifier. Although it does not directly identify you by name, pseudonymous identifiers and related technical information may still be personal information.

Independent of optional Analytics

Remote Config is enabled as an operational service. It can run before you make an Analytics choice and continues if you decline or withdraw Analytics consent. The game does not provide a separate in-game switch to disable Remote Config. This does not enable optional Firebase Analytics, grant Analytics consent or override your advertising privacy choices. You can decline optional Analytics and continue playing.

We use Remote Config to operate the advertising-availability setting. Our implementation does not use Analytics audiences, experiments, personalization or custom player signals for this setting. Remote Config does not upload the game's save file, wallet, business progression or boost timers. Changing ad availability does not remove existing boosts or change saved progress.

Firebase processes this information to provide its configuration and installation services. Google also uses aggregated technical information, such as operating-system and SDK versions, to understand service usage and improve Firebase products. Its handling of Firebase service data is described in Google's privacy information linked below.

The activated setting is cached on your device. The game also remembers the last activated advertising-availability value locally for use if a later startup check cannot complete. These configuration records are separate from your game save and Analytics consent choice.

6. Independent Analytics and Advertising Choices

Analytics consent and advertising privacy choices are independent. Accepting or declining analytics does not grant or withdraw advertising consent. Changing your advertising choices does not enable optional Firebase Analytics. To change both, use each service's privacy controls.

Analytics and advertising choices do not disable Remote Config's operational requests. Withdrawing Analytics consent stops optional Analytics collection and resets local Analytics data; it does not delete the separate Remote Config installation identifier or configuration cache.

At startup, the game requests updated consent information through UMP and presents a privacy form when required. UMP handles the consent information and choices used by Google's advertising services. The game checks both the resolved Remote Config ad setting and UMP permission before loading or displaying rewarded ads.

Settings > Privacy Options is available when UMP requires advertising privacy options or the optional Analytics privacy controls are available. Advertising options and Analytics choices are presented separately. The advertising form may not be available when UMP reports that it is not required.

Permission to request an ad does not necessarily mean that you agreed to personalized advertising. Non-personalized or limited advertising may still involve data processing. The choices available depend on the privacy message presented to you.

You can also use Android's advertising privacy controls to reset or delete your advertising ID where supported. Device controls vary by Android version and do not replace the in-game privacy choices.

Accepting the game's Terms or reading this policy is not consent to Firebase Analytics or personalized advertising. Analytics opt-in and any required advertising consent are handled separately.

9. Retention, Storage and Security

The Google Analytics event-data retention setting for this game is 2 months. This setting governs event data subject to Google's retention controls, including data used in explorations and funnel reports. Google deletes expired data on a monthly cycle; this is not a promise that every copy is deleted exactly two months after collection. Standard aggregated reports are not affected by this setting and may remain available longer. User-level data is subject to Google's applicable retention settings and policies.

AdMob and UMP data, including advertising measurement, consent information and SDK diagnostics, have separate handling and retention rules under Google's policies. The Firebase event-data setting does not set their retention periods.

Remote Config and Firebase Installations also have retention rules separate from the two-month Analytics event-data setting. Google states that Remote Config installation identifiers are retained until the Firebase customer requests deletion through the applicable API. After that request, data is removed from live and backup systems within 180 days. The game does not currently provide an in-game Remote Config installation-deletion control. Analytics withdrawal does not make this deletion request or delete Remote Config installation data. You may contact us about privacy requests using the address below.

Gameplay saves are stored locally as described above. Google handles retention and deletion of information processed through its services according to its policies and available privacy controls. Removing the game's local data does not automatically delete information already held by Google or an advertiser.

Google may process advertising, Analytics and operational Firebase information in countries other than your own. Google's policy explains its international data handling. The game does not provide a developer-operated international cloud-save service.

No device storage or network service can be guaranteed completely secure. Avoid including unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.
